Players shouldn't have to wonder if different words, situations, or actions mean the same thing.

- **Application:** If the "Spacebar" means jump, it should always mean jump. Red barrels explode. Glowing items can be picked up. Breaking these industry standards forces the player to unlearn their instincts.
    
- **Game Dev Example:** When building UI using declarative frameworks like React-Lua, keeping your component states consistent (e.g., all "Confirm" buttons are green and in the bottom right corner) is crucial for a smooth player experience.


To give you the brutal, honest truth: if you want to brute-force the algorithm using _pure ad spend_ to hold a sustained 50 CCU for 14 days straight, it requires a significant amount of money.

Earlier, I used a $300 budget as a theoretical pacing example, but let’s run the exact math for that specific 50 CCU threshold using the $0.02 Cost Per Play (CPP) and a 15-minute average session length.

### The Raw Math to Hold 50 CCU

- **Daily Plays Needed:** ~4,800 plays (to maintain 50 CCU all day)
    
- **Daily Ad Spend:** 4,800 plays × $0.02 = **$96 USD per day**
    
- **7-Day Campaign:** $96 × 7 = **$672 USD** (~RM 3,100)
    
- **14-Day Campaign:** $96 × 14 = **$1,344 USD** (~RM 6,200)
    

Dropping over RM 6,000 in two weeks is a massive, unreasonable risk for a solo student developer. The big studios can afford to bleed thousands of dollars upfront to force the algorithm’s hand, but you have to play it smarter.

Here is how you "cheat" the math so you don't actually have to spend $1,300 USD to hit that 50 CCU mark:

### 1. Double the Session Time (Cuts Cost in Half)

The math above assumes players stay for 15 minutes. If your long-term progression systems (like deep crafting trees or social roleplay elements) are incredibly engaging and players stay for **30 minutes**, your CCU naturally doubles without you buying a single extra player.

- Instead of needing 4,800 daily plays, you only need 2,400.
- Your 14-day cost instantly drops from $1,344 to **$672 USD**.

### 2. Leverage Your "Warm" Network (Free CCU)

You do not have to buy all 50 CCU. This is where you use your community as leverage. Once your $30 micro-tests prove the game isn't buggy and the monetization works, you blast the link to your campus networks, Discord groups, and the IIUM community.

- If your friends, classmates, and social network can hold a baseline of 20 to 25 CCU just by playing together in the evenings...
- You only need to run ads to buy the remaining 25 CCU.
- This drops your ad cost down to roughly **$300 to $400 USD** for the two weeks

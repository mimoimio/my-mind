This is the exact medicine for the "Day 7 Drop-Off."

The First-Time User Experience (FTUE) relies on rapid, instant gratification—click a button, get a coin, upgrade immediately. But by Day 7, that loop is boring. Long-term progression shifts the player's motivation from _instant action_ to _status, collection, and habit_.

Here are the most effective long-term progression systems on Roblox to keep players grinding for months, naturally pushing your LTV higher:

### 1. The [[Rebirth or Prestige]] System (The Infinite Loop)

This is the backbone of almost every successful Roblox tycoon or simulator. When a player maxes out their current progress, you offer them a choice: reset everything back to zero, but keep a permanent, highly visible multiplier.

### 2. The [[Collection & Gacha Chase]] (The "White Whale")

Players are hoarders. If you give them an empty index or a sticker book, they will play until it is full.

### 3. Asynchronous Social Competition ([[Leaderboards]])

Roblox players are highly competitive and driven by social status. You don't need real-time PvP combat to make a game competitive.

### 4. Habit-Building ([[Daily or Weekly Bounties]])

You need to train the player's brain to view your game as a daily routine.

### The Technical Advantage

Because you are managing state centrally with Reflex on a server-authoritative architecture, implementing these systems is highly secure. When a player "Rebirths," you aren't relying on the client to honestly report their stats. You just dispatch a state reset action on the server while incrementing their prestige multiplier, making the game virtually immune to exploiters trying to fake their long-term progression.

## 1. Feasibility Analysis & The Acquisition Concern
Your biggest concern is acquisition and the steep [[RM 6,200]] (approx $1.3k USD) requirement for cold traffic. However, based on your [[Previous Ad Acquisitions]], your situation is actually highly favorable for bootstrapping:
- **The Organic Multiplier:** On *Untitled Witchery Tycoon*, you turned 1.6k ad plays into 4,884 total visits. That is a **3x organic multiplier**. The Roblox algorithm clearly favored your game due to its retention (200+ hours played).
- **The Monetization Shift:** On *Craft Coffees!* and *Coffee Tavern*, you proved you can generate Rev (105 Robux peak) with *lower* ad spend. 

**Conclusion on Feasibility:** You do **not** need 6k MYR to start. Because you already know how to build a game that the algorithm picks up (high playtime) AND you now know how to monetize it, you are perfectly positioned to build a [[Money Printer (Profitable LTV)]] using micro-budgets and reinvestment.

## 2. Actionable Start and Direction (To Breakeven)

### Phase 1: Perfecting the Core Loop & Monetization (Weeks 1-2)
Before spending a single dollar of cold traffic, ensure your new/updated game is optimized for earnings and retention.
- **Hook them early:** Apply concepts from [[Minute1 No Reading Allowed]] to [[Minute 5 The Cliffhanger and The Pitch]]. 
- **Ensure LTV exists:** Build your [[LTV Blueprints]]. If players are staying for 200 hours, you need a sink for their Robux. (See: [[The Whale Mechanics]] and [[Rebirth or Prestige]]).

### Phase 2: Warm Traffic & University Leverage (Weeks 3-4)
You mentioned lacking reputation at uni. **You do not need reputation to get playtesters or funding.**
- **The Feedback Angle:** Instead of asking people to "play your game," ask them to "critique your project." Uni students love giving opinions. Post in your uni Discord/FB groups saying: *"I'm an indie dev trying to break into the Roblox market. I need 10 brave souls to break my game and tell me why it sucks. Free in-game perks for testers."*
- **The Goal:** This gets you your initial 15-30 CCU completely for free, which gives you telemetry data (Session length, Day-1 Retention).
- **Pitching Lecturers & Departments:** Treat your Roblox game as a legitimate startup. Approach lecturers, innovation departments, or student entrepreneurship offices with a polished proposal. Pitch them on the solid business metrics you already have (your previous 3x organic multiplier, proven monetization). They often control [[Grant Funding]], can allocate dev resources, or provide hardware/software access that completely bypasses your need for out-of-pocket expenses.

### Phase 3: The Micro-Test (Weeks 4-5)
Instead of a massive 6k MYR spend, use the earnings from *Coffee Tavern* or a strict micro-budget (e.g., $10-$20 USD).
- **Goal:** Try to hit a [[spike 50 to 100 CCU]] for a very short period.
- **Metric Check:** Measure your **ARPDAU** (Average Revenue Per Daily Active User) and compare it to your **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost). 
- **The Breakeven Rule:** If you spend 500 Robux on ads, do you make 501 Robux back over the next 7 days? If yes, you have reached breakeven. 

### Phase 4: Snowball Growth (Weeks 6+)
Once you hit that breakeven point, the game pays for its own ads.
- **Reinvest 100% of profits:** Put every Robux earned directly back into sponsors/ads.
- **Algorithm Takeover:** Keep running these 7-day mini-campaigns. You already know from *Witchery Tycoon* that 7 days of steady, small traffic is enough for the Roblox algorithm to start pushing organic recommendations. 
- Only consider looking for external [[Grant Funding]] or spending personal [[RM 6,200]] funds once the game's LTV is mathematically proven to be profitable at a small scale.

A massive portion of a profitable LTV comes from a tiny percentage of players who have large amounts of Robux to spend (often called "whales").

- You need an infinite money sink. If a player wants to spend 10,000 Robux today, does your game let them?
    
- This is usually achieved through gacha mechanics (e.g., spinning for a 1% chance at a legendary neon coffee cup or a mythic witch hat) or a leaderboard system where players constantly spend Robux to buy currency just to stay at the #1 spot on the server.

If you can get a player to stay for 3 days and spend 15 Robux total, and your ad cost (CPP) is $0.02 (about 6 Robux), your LTV is vastly outperforming your ad spend.

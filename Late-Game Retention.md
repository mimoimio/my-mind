The Day 7 Drop-Off (Late-Game Retention)

The FTUE (First-Time User Experience) guarantees your Day 1 retention. It ensures players survive the first five minutes. But you missed what happens next.

If your game relies on a core loop—like brewing coffee, serving customers, or upgrading a tycoon—the FTUE gets them hooked today. But why do they log in next Tuesday?

- **The Missing Piece:** You need [[long-term progression systems]]. This means daily login streaks, weekly leaderboards, or a prestige system where they reset their progress for a massive permanent multiplier. If there is no late-game goal, your Day 7 retention will flatline, dragging your LTV down with it.

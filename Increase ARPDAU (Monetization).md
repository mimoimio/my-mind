Once players are sticking around, you need to seamlessly integrate spending into the core loop without making it feel like a cash grab. The key here is relying heavily on repeatable **Developer Products**, not just one-time Gamepasses.

- **Monetize the Wait Time (Time = Money):** If the core gameplay involves waiting—such as waiting for a complex brew to finish on a tycoon dropper or waiting for a specific ingredient to process—sell a cheap, 10-Robux product to instantly finish the timer.
    
- **The "Mistake Eraser":** If a player accidentally ruins a procedural crafting step or burns a recipe they spent five minutes preparing, offer a small Robux prompt to "Save the Batch." Players will gladly pay a few cents of real-world money to save their hard work.
    
- **Social "Flex" Spending:** In social environments like a bustling tavern or cafe, players want to be recognized. Allow them to buy a server-wide "Happy Hour" that doubles everyone's earnings for 10 minutes, complete with a global chat announcement with their name. You can also let them drop a physical Tip Jar on a counter where other players can donate directly to them.
    
- **The High-Value Starter Pack:** Hit them with a bundle the second they finish the tutorial. Offer a unique cosmetic (like an exclusive barista apron or a glowing cauldron effect) bundled with some starter cash for 49 Robux. It converts cold ad traffic into paying users immediately on Day.

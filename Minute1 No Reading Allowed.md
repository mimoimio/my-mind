Players hate reading text walls. When they spawn in, your UI needs to be instantly intuitive and completely stripped down.

- **The Action:** The screen should only display what they need _right now_. Use a glowing arrow, a subtle particle trail, or a highlighted UI button to direct them to the very first action—like grabbing a base ingredient or an empty cup.
    
- **The Rule:** Do not explain the lore. Do not show them the premium shop. Just get the first object into their hands.

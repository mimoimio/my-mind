Minimize the player's memory load by making objects, actions, and options visible.

- **Application:** Quest logs, minimaps, and compass markers. Instead of forcing a player to remember that "The blacksmith is three streets down and to the left," give them a map pin.
    
- **Game Dev Example:** In complex games like _Monster Hunter_, monsters telegraph their heavy attacks with specific animations. The player _recognizes_ the animation and dodges, rather than having to _recall_ a hidden internal timer.

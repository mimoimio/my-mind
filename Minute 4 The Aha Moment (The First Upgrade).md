This is where the hook sets in. They have money, and you are going to force them to spend it immediately.

The keyword to focus on: [[Acceleration from Velocity]]

- **The Action:** Guide them directly to the upgrade menu or physical button. They purchase their first expansion—perhaps unlocking a new flavored syrup, a faster brewing cauldron, or an automated dropper.
    
- **The Impact:** The upgrade must have an immediate, visible impact. The machine should look cooler, the UI should expand, or their movement speed should visibly increase. They need to instantly understand: _Play game -> Get money -> Game gets better._

When you run the same ad thumbnail for four weeks straight, you will hit Ad Fatigue.
Roblox's system will eventually show your ad to the same people multiple times. By Week 3, your Click-Through Rate (CTR) will naturally start to drop, meaning your Cost Per Play (CPP) will go up.

The Fix: You must have at least three different thumbnails and ad copies ready. When you see your CTR dip at the end of Week 2, immediately swap to a fresh thumbnail to reset the audience's attention.

How?:
- [[Hitting the algorithm]]. When the Roblox algorithm picks up your game and puts it on the "Recommended" or "Up-and-Coming" page, it feeds you thousands of players for free.
	- **How it makes money:** When a player finds your game organically through the algorithm, your Cost Per Play (CPP) for that specific user is **$0.00**.
    
	- **The Math:** Because you spent nothing to acquire them, every single Robux they spend is 100% pure profit margin. Even if your game has a terrible LTV—let's say players only spend an average of 1 Robux before quitting—if the algorithm feeds you 100,000 players, you just made 100,000 Robux for free.

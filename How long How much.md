Roblox algorithm does not actually care about a massive, one-day spike. It cares about **sustained velocity**.

If you spend $200 in a single day, push your game to 500 CCU, and then turn the ads off, the algorithm will completely ignore you. It assumes the spike was artificial (or a YouTuber played it) and will wait to see if the players come back. When they don't naturally sustain those numbers on Day 2, your game is dead in the water.

To trigger the discovery algorithm (which puts you on the Home page recommendations), here are the exact numbers and durations you need to hit:

[[7-14 days straight]]
[[spike 50 to 100 CCU]]
[[RM 6,200]]

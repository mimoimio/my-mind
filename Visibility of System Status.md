Players need to know what is happening in the game world at all times without guessing.

- **Application:** Health bars, stamina gauges, ammo counters, and cooldown timers. If a player is under a status effect (like poison or a buff), there should be a clear visual or audio cue.
- **Game Dev Example:** If you're building a potion-brewing mechanic, the cauldron should visually bubble or change color to indicate it's processing, rather than leaving the player wondering if they clicked the button correctly.

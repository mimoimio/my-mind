Because the algorithm needs time, the worst thing you can do is burn your entire budget on a weekend. You need to spread your budget out to trick the algorithm into thinking your game is growing organically.

Here is the exact pacing strategy to use once you have completed your $30 micro-tests and verified your LTV is profitable:

1. **The Budget:** Assume you have a total scaling budget of $300 USD.
    
2. **The Daily Cap:** Instead of running it all at once, set a strict daily cap of **$20 to $25 per day**.
    
3. **The 14-Day Burn:** Run the ads consistently every single day for two weeks.

### What Happens Behind the Scenes

- **Days 1–4:** You are buying your 50–100 CCU. The algorithm is watching, logging your average session time (aim for 10+ minutes) and your Day 1 retention (aim for 20%+). You get exactly zero free players.
    
- **Days 5–7:** If your metrics are holding up, the algorithm starts testing you. It will drip-feed you a tiny amount of organic impressions (maybe 50 to 100 extra free plays a day) just to see if non-ad players like the game too.
    
- **Days 8–14:** This is where the magic happens. The algorithm validates your 7-day retention. Because your ads are _still_ running and pushing that 50-100 CCU, the system registers "consistent upward momentum." It opens the floodgates. Your Home Page impressions will suddenly spike, and your CCU will organically jump from 100 to 300+.

So how?:
To get a profitable LTV, you have to attack the mathematical formula from both sides. Since **LTV = ARPDAU (Daily Spend) × Lifespan (Retention)**, you need to make players stay longer _and_ give them compelling reasons to spend repeatedly while they are there.

You need to utilize [[LTV Blueprints]] to bring your money printer running.

But these blueprints are useless without a good [[First-Time User Experience]] (FTUE) is the linchpin that holds the entire blueprint together. To put it simply: **If your FTUE fails, your LTV is zero.**
